## 场景1：谎言自证

### 场景氛围
- 时间: 深夜
- 情绪: 紧张、绝望、赌命
- 光源: 青灯（暖黄）+ 月光（冷蓝）

---

### 镜头 2-1-1：辛月影声泪俱下

**特效组合**: 青灯光晕 + 月光光束 + 泪滴反光

**特效提示词**:
```
flickering blue oil lamp casting warm dying glow,
cold moonlight streaming through torn window paper creating light rays,
volumetric lighting, dust particles floating in light beams,
teardrops catching candlelight, glistening on cheeks,
dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, warm candlelight on one side of face, cold moonlight on other,
dim atmospheric lighting, cinematic shadows
```

**特效强度**: 中等 (moderate)

**实施要点**:
- 青灯火焰微弱，产生摇曳的光晕
- 月光从破窗照入，形成丁达尔效应（光束）
- 泪滴在青灯下闪闪发光
- 光影分割：一边暖光（青灯），一边冷光（月光）

---

### 镜头 2-1-2：辛月影恳切的脸

**特效组合**: 泪滴特写光效 + 背景虚化光斑

**特效提示词**:
```
extreme close-up on tears, crystal teardrops catching light,
bokeh background with warm candlelight orbs,
tears glistening like pearls, single tear rolling down cheek,
shallow depth of field, soft focus background,
emotional lighting emphasizing tear-streaked face
```

**特效强度**: 中等 (moderate)

**实施要点**:
- 泪滴特写，捕捉光线反射
- 背景虚化成光斑
- 单滴泪水滑落的瞬间

---

### 镜头 2-1-3：沈清起眼眸微颤

**特效组合**: 阴影分割 + 眼眸微光

**特效提示词**:
```
dramatic split lighting, half face in shadow, half in dim candlelight,
subtle highlight in phoenix eyes, eye reflection catching faint light,
ice-like cold atmosphere, frozen surface hiding surging waves beneath,
tense and suspenseful atmosphere, moody cinematic lighting
```

**特效强度**: 微妙 (subtle)

**实施要点**:
- 脸部一半在阴影中，一半在微光中
- 凤眼中有一丝微弱的反光
- 营造"冰封之下暗藏波涛"的感觉

---

### 镜头 2-1-4：霍齐夺弓冲出

**特效组合**: 动态模糊 + 门框光线变化

**特效提示词**:
```

doorway light spill, figure crossing from dim interior to outdoor moonlight,
fast action blur, urgency in movement,
bow and arrow silhouette against moonlight
```

**特效强度**: 中等 (moderate)

**实施要点**:
- 霍齐奔跑的动态模糊

- 弓箭剪影

---

### 镜头 2-2-3：血滴落地 ⭐⭐⭐

**特效组合**: 血滴慢动作 + 凄美光效 + 荼蘼意象（本章核心特效）

**特效提示词**:
```
extreme close-up macro shot, slow motion blood drops falling,
crimson red blood drops glowing translucent in dim candlelight,
each drop like a crimson tear falling in slow motion,
blood drops landing on ground creating small splatter patterns,
poetic imagery of fallen pear blossoms (tumi flowers),
shallow depth of field, blood drops in sharp focus,
background blurred into soft bokeh,
beautiful tragedy, visual metaphor for hidden pain,
cinematic slow motion effect, 120fps aesthetic
```

**特效强度**: 强烈 (intense)

**实施要点**:
- **慢动作效果**: 血滴下落速度降至正常的1/4
- **光效**: 血滴在微光中呈半透明的猩红色
- **特写**: 聚焦血滴坠落的瞬间
- **意象**: 血滴落地溅开，如荼蘼花开
- **景深**: 血滴清晰，背景虚化
- **色彩**: 猩红与暗褐的对比

**技术参数**:
- 慢动作速度: 25%（正常速度的1/4）
- 色调增强: 猩红色饱和度+20%
- 景深: 浅景深，f/1.8效果

---
